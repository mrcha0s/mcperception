# Color Palette — Complete Hex Reference

## IMPORTANT
These are the ONLY permitted colors in the entire design system. Using ANY color not listed here is a violation of the design system.

### Special Colors
| Name  | Hex       | Usage                     |
|-------|-----------|---------------------------|
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Mode 1 surface, text      |
| Black | `#0A0A0A` | Mode 2 surface, text      |

> Note: Pure `#000000` is NOT used. Always use `#0A0A0A` for black.

## Luminance Curve (Universal)

All 7 hues share identical luminance structure:

```
Step 50:  lum ≈ 0.950   (near-white)
Step 100: lum ≈ 0.750
Step 200: lum ≈ 0.550
Step 300: lum ≈ 0.350
Step 400: lum ≈ 0.212   (mid — fails AA small on white, passes on black)
Step 500: lum ≈ 0.150   (mid-dark — passes AA small on white at ~5.2:1)
Step 600: lum ≈ 0.082   (dark — strong contrast on white at ~8.0:1)
Step 700: lum ≈ 0.037
Step 800: lum ≈ 0.016
Step 900: lum ≈ 0.003   (near-black)
```
