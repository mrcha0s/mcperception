Components / Button

Button

Surface-mode-aware interactive action triggers. 7 hues, 4 variants, 6 sizes, 5 surface modes.

Quick Start
<!-- Basic usage --> <button class="mc-button mc-button-filled" data-hue="blue">Submit</button> <!-- With size --> <button class="mc-button mc-button-outline mc-button-lg" data-hue="green">Approve</button> <!-- Surface mode adaptation (inherits from parent) --> <div data-hue="neutral" data-mode="1"> <button class="mc-button mc-button-filled" data-hue="red">Delete</button> </div>

4 Surface Mode Adaptation

Buttons auto-adapt to the parent data-mode context.

M1 — White #FFFFFF
M2 — Black #0A0A0A
M3 — Light Blue #DEDEFF
M4 — Dark Blue #000081
M5 — Mid Blue #5252FF
<!-- Buttons auto-adapt via ancestor data-mode --> <div data-hue="blue" data-mode="1"> <!-- white surface --> <button class="mc-button mc-button-filled" data-hue="blue">Adapts</button> </div> <div data-hue="blue" data-mode="5"> <!-- mid surface — fills invert --> <button class="mc-button mc-button-filled" data-hue="blue">Inverted</button> </div>
