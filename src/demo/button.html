Button

Surface-mode-aware interactive action triggers. 7 hues, 4 variants, 6 sizes, 5 surface modes.

Quick Start
<!-- Basic usage --> <button class="mc-button mc-button-filled" data-hue="blue">Submit</button> <!-- With size --> <button class="mc-button mc-button-outline mc-button-lg" data-hue="green">Approve</button> <!-- Surface mode adaptation (inherits from parent) --> <div data-hue="neutral" data-mode="1"> <button class="mc-button mc-button-filled" data-hue="red">Delete</button> </div>

1 Variants

Four emphasis levels for action hierarchy.

Filled — High Emphasis
<button class="mc-button mc-button-filled" data-hue="blue">Primary</button>
Tonal — Medium-High Emphasis
<button class="mc-button mc-button-tonal" data-hue="blue">Tonal</button>
Outline — Medium Emphasis
<button class="mc-button mc-button-outline" data-hue="blue">Outline</button>
Ghost — Low Emphasis
<button class="mc-button mc-button-ghost" data-hue="blue">Ghost</button>
