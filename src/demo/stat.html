Stat

KPI and metric display blocks for dashboards and data-rich UIs. Supports icons, descriptions, action buttons, and centered alignment. 7 hues, 6 sizes, 5 surface modes, and WCAG AA compliance.

Display WCAG AA 7 Hues 5 Modes
Quick Start
<div class="mc-stats" data-hue="blue"> <div class="mc-stat"> <div class="mc-stat-title">Total Revenue</div> <div class="mc-stat-value">$45,231</div> <div class="mc-stat-desc">+20.1% from last month</div> </div> </div>
