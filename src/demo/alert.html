Alert

Surface-mode-aware notification banners, callouts, and inline messages. 7 hues, 3 variants, 6 sizes, 5 surface modes.

New API WCAG AA 7 Hues 5 Modes
Quick Start
<!-- Basic alert (soft) --> <div class="mc-alert" data-hue="blue"> <div class="mc-alert-content">This is a blue alert.</div> </div> <!-- Filled variant --> <div class="mc-alert mc-alert-filled" data-hue="green"> <div class="mc-alert-content">Success! Your changes have been saved.</div> </div> <!-- On a white surface --> <div data-mode="1"> <div class="mc-alert mc-alert-outline" data-hue="red"> <div class="mc-alert-content">Error: Something went wrong.</div> </div> </div>
