Progress

Surface-mode-aware progress bars, skill meters, and loading tracks. 7 hues, 6 sizes, 5 surface modes. Supports labels, stripes, animation, and stacked bars.

New API WCAG AA 7 Hues 5 Modes
Quick Start
<!-- Basic progress bar --> <div class="mc-progress" data-hue="blue" role="progressbar" aria-valuenow="60" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100"> <div class="mc-progress-bar" style="width: 60%"></div> </div> <!-- With label --> <div class="mc-progress mc-progress-lg" data-hue="green" role="progressbar" aria-valuenow="75" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100"> <div class="mc-progress-bar" style="width: 75%">75%</div> </div>
