Spinner

Surface-mode-aware loading indicators. Border spinners and grow spinners with 7 hues, 6 sizes, 5 surface modes. Accessible with prefers-reduced-motion support.

Animated WCAG AA 7 Hues 5 Modes
Quick Start
<!-- Border spinner --> <div class="mc-spinner-border" data-hue="blue" role="status"> <span class="sr-only">Loading...</span> </div> <!-- Grow spinner --> <div class="mc-spinner-grow" data-hue="green" role="status"> <span class="sr-only">Loading...</span> </div>
