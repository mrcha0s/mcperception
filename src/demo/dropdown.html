Dropdowns

Contextual overlay menus with items, headers, dividers, and directional variants. 7 hues, 6 sizes, 5 surface modes with WCAG AA compliance.

Interactive WCAG AA 7 Hues 5 Modes

4 Directions

Dropdowns can open in four directions using modifier classes: down (default), up, end (right), and start (left). Center-aligned menus are also supported.

6 Surface Modes

Every dropdown must render correctly on all 5 surface modes. The dropdown inherits its mode from the parent data-mode attribute.

7 Semantic Mapping

Dropdowns with semantic hue assignments. Use the correct hue to communicate intent: blue for primary, green for success, red for danger, yellow for warning, teal for info, magenta for premium.
