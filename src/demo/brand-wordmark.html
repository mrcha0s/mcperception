Wordmark

The MC Perception brand identity: the MC monogram paired with the Perception wordmark, set in Roboto Slab. Slab serifs give the mark a precise, engineered character that mirrors the strict, measured nature of the system itself.

Roboto Slab Wordmark Weights 600–900

MC Perception Design System

1 Anatomy of the Lockup

The full lockup combines two elements: the MC monogram (a square tile, Roboto Slab Black 900) and the Perception wordmark (Roboto Slab Bold 700). An optional condensed tagline anchors the baseline. Everything is built from the font stack var(--font-slab).

2 The Monogram

The MC monogram is the compact mark — used for favicons, avatars, app icons, and tight spaces where the full wordmark will not fit. It is always a square tile with a corner radius of 3px (depth-token limit for dark surfaces). Default treatment is white on blue.500.

4 Clear Space & Minimum Size

Give the mark room to breathe. Reserve clear space equal to the height of the monogram tile (1×) on every side. Never crowd the lockup with text, rules, or other logos inside this margin.

5 Roboto Slab Weight Specimen

The wordmark draws on the full Roboto Slab range. SemiBold (600) is now available and bridges the gap between the body-weight Regular and the wordmark Bold. The monogram always uses Black (900) for maximum presence at small sizes.
