Color Picker

An interactive sampler for the entire palette — 7 hues × 10 steps plus the white and black anchors. Click any swatch to read its hex, token, and utility classes, and copy the hex to your clipboard. Pick by role + step, never a free hex.

3 Which Step on Which Surface

The picker shows raw swatches, but the surface mode dictates which step is safe. These rules are universal across all 7 hues.

Mode 1 — White Surface
Text: Steps 500-900
Accent: Step 500
Divider: Step 400
Avoid: Steps 50-300 for text
Mode 2 — Black Surface
Text: Steps 50-400 + White
Accent: Step 400
Divider: Step 500
Avoid: Steps 600-900 for text
Mode 3 — Light Hue (100)
Text: Steps 600-900 + Black
Accent: Step 600
Divider: Step 500
Avoid: Steps 50-300 for text
Mode 4 — Dark Hue (800)
Text: Steps 50-300 + White
Accent: Step 300
Divider: Step 500
Avoid: Steps 600-900 for text
Mode 5 — Mid Hue (500)
Text: White / Step 50
Accent: Step 50
Divider: Black
Avoid: Steps 300-700 for text
WCAG Thresholds
4.5:1 — AA small text
3.0:1 — AA large text / UI
<3.0:1 — Fails
