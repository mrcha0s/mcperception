Semantic Mapping

Reach for the intent, never the raw color. Seven semantic roles map to seven engineered hues, so a theme stays consistent, accessible, and re-skinnable across every component.

7 Roles AA Verified Re-skinnable
QUICK START
<!-- Use the role's hue, not a hand-picked hex -->
<button class="mc-button mc-button-filled" data-hue="blue">Primary</button>
<button class="mc-button mc-button-filled" data-hue="red">Delete</button>

<!-- Status badges -->
<span class="mc-badge mc-badge-filled" data-hue="green">Success</span>
<span class="mc-badge mc-badge-filled" data-hue="yellow">Pending</span>

Why intent, not color?
A single data-hue="green" swap re-themes every success state at once.
Surfaces choose the correct step automatically — you never pick a step by hand.

4 Reach For The Role

Always express intent through the semantic hue. Hand-picked hex values break theming, drift out of the palette, and can silently fail WCAG.

DO — semantic hue
<button class="mc-button mc-button-filled"
       data-hue="red">
  Delete account
</button>
DON'T — hand-picked hex
<button style="background:#e23b3b">
  Delete account
</button>
/* off-palette, unthemed, may fail AA */
